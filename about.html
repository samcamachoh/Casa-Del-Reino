BienvenidoWelcome

Bienvenido a casaWelcome home

Casa Del Reino nació con una visión sencilla: construir una casa donde las familias puedan encontrar amor real, una comunidad genuina, y un camino claro de transformación a través de Jesús. No importa de dónde vengas o qué temporada estés viviendo — aquí hay un lugar para ti.

Casa Del Reino was built around a simple vision: a house where families can find real love, genuine community, and a clear path of transformation through Jesus. No matter where you're coming from or what season you're in, there's a place for you here.

Love

Before any program or event, we believe in the power of a love that receives people exactly as they are.

We are a multigenerational family — kids, youth, parents, and grandparents growing together in faith.

Transformation

We believe every person can be transformed by the power of God, one step at a time.

Visión y MisiónVision & Mission

Visión

Vision

Ser una casa donde las personas encuentren a Cristo, sean transformadas por Su presencia, equipadas para cumplir su propósito y enviadas para extender el Reino de Dios.

To be a house where people encounter Christ, are transformed by His presence, equipped to fulfill their purpose, and sent out to extend God's Kingdom.

Misión

Mission

Rescatar personas, formar discípulos maduros, desarrollar líderes y establecer familias fuertes que reflejen el Reino de Dios en cada esfera de la sociedad.

To rescue people, form mature disciples, develop leaders, and establish strong families that reflect God's Kingdom in every sphere of society.

Declaración de Identidad

Identity Statement

Casa del Reino existe para levantar una familia espiritual que ame a Dios, ame a las personas y transforme su mundo.

Casa del Reino exists to raise up a spiritual family that loves God, loves people, and transforms their world.
